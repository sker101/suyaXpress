A NEW CULINARY EXPRESSION

Dar es Salaam is about to experience African flavours through a different lens.

Suya Express is a new culinary expression created by Chef Victore, bringing together the bold fire and spice of West African cooking with the depth, character and soul of East African cuisine.

Chef Victore is regarded as one of Tanzania's finest Taste Masters, drawing on years of experience across hospitality management and culinary innovation. Every plate takes its cue from the traditional West African art of cooking over open fire, reinterpreted for the modern Dar es Salaam diner.

More than a food outlet, Suya Express is an expression of African food culture through contemporary hospitality — bold, approachable, and rooted in authenticity.
